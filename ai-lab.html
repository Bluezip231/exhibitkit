AI Review Lab

AI Review Lab uses local AI to help organize messages by relevance. It does not decide whether evidence is admissible, truthful, or legally sufficient.

Please read first.

AI Review Lab is a private review helper. It does not provide legal advice and does not decide whether evidence is admissible, truthful, or legally sufficient. It only helps organize selected text by possible relevance. Always review the original messages yourself.

Your messages stay in your browser. The AI model may download to your device, but your message text is not uploaded to an AI API. Nothing is saved - not in the cloud, not in your browser storage. Refresh the page and it is gone.

The official ExhibitKit exhibit builder and the court-ready PDFs it produces stay deterministic and AI-free. This lab is a separate, optional helper - it never rewrites your evidence or changes the exhibit PDF.

How AI Review Lab works (and its limits)

  • It loads a small sentence-embedding model into your browser and measures the semantic relevance between your claim and each message. This is not a "legal score" or "case strength."
  • It groups messages into Strongly related, Possibly related, Needs context and Low match - and never deletes anything.
  • It may miss relevant messages, and it may rank irrelevant messages highly. Short replies like "yes" or "that works" usually need surrounding context.
  • It does not decide admissibility, truthfulness, or legal sufficiency. Human review is required.

ExhibitKit formats records and computes integrity hashes. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. AI Review Lab is for organization and review only.

Support ExhibitKit

ExhibitKit is free, with no accounts, no ads, and no upsells — and it stays that way. The most valuable support costs nothing:

Free ways to help

  • Tell someone who needs it. Most people only find a tool like this in a stressful moment. If you know someone heading to family court, small claims, or dealing with a scam, send them the link.
  • Star or share the project on GitHub. It helps others find it and trust it.
  • Report a bug or rough edge. If something didn't parse, didn't look right, or confused you, tell us on the contact page or open a GitHub issue. Real-world export quirks are exactly what makes the tool better.
  • Suggest an improvement. Missing a format or a feature? We'd like to hear it.

Chip in toward hosting

If ExhibitKit saved you money or stress and you'd like to give back, a small contribution helps cover hosting and keeps the tool free for people who can't pay.

Donation link coming soon

Donations are entirely optional and never unlock features — every part of ExhibitKit is free for everyone.
